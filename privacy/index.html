Privacy Policy

Cooking Log does not collect, store, or sell your personal data on our servers. We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs.

Your cooking records

Dishes, ingredient amounts, photos, ratings, and notes are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, SwiftData syncs them through CloudKit to your private iCloud database. That database belongs to your Apple ID. We cannot read it.

If iCloud is off

Records stay on that device. Signing into iCloud later lets the on-device CloudKit store sync. The app does not upload records to any server we operate.

Export and import

Export writes a file you choose (Markdown, JSON, or CSV). Import reads a JSON file you choose. Those files stay where you save them.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your card number. Apple tells the app whether this Apple ID has unlocked Premium.

隐私政策

菜记不会在我们的服务器上收集、存储或出售你的个人数据。我们不使用分析和广告 SDK。

做菜记录

菜名、用量、照片、评分和备注保存在你的设备上。iCloud 可用时，SwiftData 通过 CloudKit 同步到你的 iCloud 私有数据库。这个库属于你的 Apple ID，我们无法读取。

没有登录 iCloud 时

记录留在这台设备上。之后登录 iCloud，本机的 CloudKit 存储会开始同步。App 不会把记录上传到我们自己的服务器。

导出和导入

导出把 Markdown、JSON 或 CSV 写到你选择的位置。导入读取你选择的 JSON。文件留在你保存的地方。

购买

高级版由 Apple 处理一次性付款。我们收不到银行卡号。Apple 只告诉我们这个 Apple ID 是否已解锁高级版。
