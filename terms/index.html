Cooking Log is licensed, not sold. You may use it on Apple devices under your Apple ID, following the App Store terms.

Your records

You own the cooking notes you enter. Keep a JSON export if you want a copy outside iCloud. Import of that JSON is free.

Premium

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks export to Markdown, JSON, and CSV. Recording, editing, categories, ratings, photos, and iCloud sync do not require Premium. The price is shown by the App Store before you pay. Restore Purchases moves the unlock to another device on the same Apple ID. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Availability

iCloud sync needs Apple’s iCloud service and a signed-in Apple ID. We do not guarantee that a particular dish, photo, or export will be preserved if you delete the app, leave iCloud, or lose the device without a backup.
