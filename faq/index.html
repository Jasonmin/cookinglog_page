FAQ

Why iCloud instead of a folder of files?

A cooking log is structured: a dish, many ingredient lines, steps, a rating, and a result. A database syncs those pieces without merging two edited text files by hand. Export still gives you Markdown, JSON, and CSV when you want a file.

Does sync require Premium?

No. iCloud sync, unlimited records, categories, ratings, and photos are free. Premium is only export.

What if I am not signed into iCloud?

The app still saves on this device and says so in Settings. Sign in, then leave the app open on a network for a while.

What does success rate count?

Success divided by every record. “Needs work” and “Failed” are not successes. The rating is separate, from 1 to 5.

Can I hide a built-in category?

Yes, in Categories. Dishes already in it keep that category. New dishes use the categories you still see.

I bought Premium on my iPhone. Where is it on my Mac?

Settings → Restore Purchases, with the same Apple ID. The iPhone and Mac apps share the bundle ID com.qzmtree.cookinglog.

常见问题

为什么用 iCloud，而不是一个文件夹？

做菜记录是结构化的：一道菜、多行材料、步骤、评分和结果。数据库同步这些内容，不用手工合并两份被同时改过的文本。需要文件时，仍然可以导出 Markdown、JSON 和 CSV。

同步要买高级版吗？

不要。iCloud 同步、无限记录、分类、评分和照片都免费。高级版只负责导出。

没有登录 iCloud 呢？

App 仍然保存在这台设备上，设置里会说明。登录之后，在有网络时让 App 开一会儿。

成功率怎么算？

成功次数除以全部记录。「待改进」和「失败」不算成功。评分是另一项，1 到 5 分。

可以隐藏内置分类吗？

可以，在分类里隐藏。已经用这个分类的菜会保留。新菜只能选你还看得到的分类。

iPhone 上买了高级版，Mac 上没有？

用同一个 Apple ID，在设置里点恢复购买。iPhone 和 Mac 的 Bundle ID 都是 com.qzmtree.cookinglog。
