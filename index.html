Write down what you cooked.

Cooking Log keeps the dish, the date, the ingredient amounts, a 1–5 rating, and whether it worked. The same records sync through your private iCloud database on iPhone and Mac.

  • Add a record with ingredients, steps, a photo, and a category
  • See the ratio when every ingredient uses the same unit
  • Scale a recipe to another number of servings
  • Premium, a one-time purchase, exports Markdown, JSON, and CSV
  • Import a JSON backup for free, so your notes are not locked in
